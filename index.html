Syllify

Four syllabi in, one honest read on your semester

Syllify reads every syllabus you were handed and pulls out the assignments, readings and exams. Then it tells you what no single syllabus can: which weeks are heaviest, where three deadlines land inside seventy-two hours, which days carry more than one thing, and how much of your grade sits in the final three weeks. Every date links back to the line it came from, marked read, inferred or guessed. Deliverables with no date get their own list instead of being dropped. Then the whole term exports as an .ics or CSV calendar.

How it works

Upload one or more syllabi as PDF or text, or paste the schedule section. The text is extracted and parsed in your browser. Review the deadlines, correct anything that looks wrong, then add them to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

Privacy

PDF text extraction and parsing run entirely in your browser. No syllabus is uploaded to a server, and there is no account or sign-in.

Limitations

  • This is a working prototype, not a finished product.
  • Text-based PDFs work. Scanned images have no text layer and will not parse.
  • Syllabi dated only by teaching week or lecture number need your term start date.
  • Confidence scores are a ranking, not a calibrated probability. Verify anything that matters.
